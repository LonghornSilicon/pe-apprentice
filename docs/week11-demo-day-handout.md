# Week 11: Demo Day

*Week 11 of 11. Handout.*

**Goal:** Present the full journey, spec to GDS, and hand off a complete package.

**Task**
Prepare a short presentation covering your PE's path through every stage: the
spec from week 2, the RTL from week 3, your golden model and the defects you
found in the vendor PE from weeks 4 and 5, your synthesis numbers from weeks 6
and 7, your floorplan and routed layout from weeks 8 and 9, and your signed off
GDS from week 10.

Two things to include that are easy to skip. One prediction you got wrong and
what you learned from being wrong: the week 6 critical path, the week 8 wire
length, the accumulator width, any of them. And one number you can defend
completely, meaning you can say where it came from, which file, which corner,
and why it is that value and not another.

**Deliverable**
- A short presentation, 5 to 10 minutes
- A final package: every deliverable from weeks 1 through 10, assembled and organized in one submission

**Done means**
- Presentation actually covers every stage, not just the last one you finished
- Final package is complete, every week's deliverable is present and easy to find
