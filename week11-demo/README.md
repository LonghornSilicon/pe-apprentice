# Week 11: Demo Day

**Goal:** Present the whole journey, spec to GDS, and hand off a complete
package.

## Do

Prepare a 5 to 10 minute presentation covering your PE's path through every
stage: the spec from week 2, the RTL from week 3, the bugs you found in week 5,
your synthesis numbers from week 7, your floorplan and routed layout from weeks
8 and 9, and your signed off GDS from week 10.

Two things to include that are easy to skip:

- **One prediction you got wrong**, and what you learned. Your week 6 critical
  path, your week 8 wire length, either one.
- **One number you can defend completely.** Meaning you can say where it came
  from, which file, which corner, and why it is that value and not another.

If you took week 10 Part C all the way and actually closed timing, show the
before and after and walk us through what you changed. That is the strongest
thing anyone can present, because it means you went back through the whole flow
a second time with a reason.

## Turn in

- The presentation
- A final package: every deliverable from weeks 1 through 10, organised in one
  submission

## Done means

- The presentation covers every stage, not just the last one you finished
- The package is complete and easy to find things in
